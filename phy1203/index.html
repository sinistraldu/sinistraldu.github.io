How to use this site

Read, play, practise

  1. Read the ideaEach class starts with the big idea in plain words, then the key formula in a box.
  2. Play with the simulationEvery simulation works the same way: a picture, a few sliders, a box of live numbers, and a green "What to notice" line.
  3. Turn the 3D viewsSimulations marked 3D can be dragged with a mouse or finger to look from any side.
  4. Try the problemsEach class ends with practice problems. Work them out first, then press Show solution.
Units

Six units, 24 classes

Unit 1 · Classes 1–5

Rotation, Momentum and Energy

Angular motion, torque, moment of inertia, angular momentum, impulse, work and rotational energy. Joints, centrifuges, molecular motors.

10 simulations · 1 in 3D
 Unit 2 · Classes 6–10

Fluids in Living Systems

Pressure, Pascal, buoyancy, continuity, Bernoulli, turbulence, Poiseuille, Stokes and diffusion. Blood flow and IV drips.

10 simulations · 1 in 3D
 Unit 3 · Classes 11–12

Sound, Ultrasound and Hearing

Vibration and waves, v = fλ, ultrasound and infrasound, echo imaging, decibels, hearing aids, ultrasonic cleaning.

8 simulations · 1 in 3D

9 simulations · 1 in 3D

Course outcomes

  1. Explain rotational motion, momentum, work and energy, fluids, electricity, magnetism, acoustics and imaging in biological and mechanical systems.
  2. Demonstrate the physical laws of rotation, fluids, waves, electric fields and potentials, RC circuits and resonance through derivations and graphs.
  3. Evaluate and solve numerical problems on torque, impulse, energy, fluid flow, fields, circuits and waves, with biological relevance.
  4. Apply EM oscillations, NMR and optics (X-rays, lasers, microscopes) to diagnostics and medical imaging.
  5. Interpret sound, ultrasound and infrasound and their use in hearing and in devices such as hearing aids and sonography.
